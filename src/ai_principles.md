# AI Principles

## AI Role

AI modules are:
- advisory
- interpretive
- hypothesis-generating

AI modules are NOT:
- autonomous traders
- final decision makers
- sources of truth

---

## Constraints

AI outputs must:
- be explainable
- include reasoning
- expose uncertainty
- cite source inputs
- remain reviewable

AI must never:
- fabricate financial data
- override deterministic core logic
- hide assumptions

---

## Evaluation

AI outputs must be:
- replayable
- logged
- benchmarkable
- critiqueable

---

## AI Confidence

AI confidence is NOT:
- probability of returns
- certainty

It is:
- internal confidence in reasoning consistency
